Who we serve

Economic Development Organizations, Foundations, and Research Parks

A university-linked innovation pipeline for your region, with the partnership terms and the capital decision written down.

Where you might be

You want your region's universities, hospitals, employers, and founders to work as one pipeline, and you want to know which investment makes that happen first: a program, a microgrant pool, a fund, a space, or a partnership agreement. You may be an economic development organization, a foundation, a research park, an organization like them, or a coalition of several.

Questions worth asking
  • What should we fund first to create a pipeline of ventures in our region?
  • How do we structure a partnership with the local university or health system?
  • Should we start or anchor a regional fund, and what would it need to succeed?
  • How do we measure the return on an innovation investment to our board and funders?

When this fits

  • 01A board or funder has set aside money for innovation and wants a plan before it is spent.
  • 02Universities and employers in your region are each running their own programs and you want them connected.
  • 03A fund has been proposed and you want an independent view of the pipeline behind it.
  • 04A federal or state program fits your region and you want the strategy in place before the application.

What we build with you

Each item maps to a module with named deliverables.

  • Regional ecosystem and founder research
  • Innovation strategy and investment sequence
  • Partnership term framework with universities, health systems, and employers
  • Venture, microgrant, or fund decision memo with options
  • Grant landscape and readiness map
  • KPI scorecard for funders and boards
Sample engagement

A typical starting engagement

Diagnostic, then Stand It Up or Build2 to 3 weeks, then 6 weeks to 6 months

Research and a roadmap first, then the one or three modules that matter most: often the partnership framework, the capital decision memo, and the scorecard funders will ask for.

Regional innovation work succeeds when the institutions that produce founders and the organizations that fund them agree on a sequence. We design that sequence with you: who does what, which investment comes first, how the partnership terms read, and how results are reported back to the people who paid for them. Your institutions run the programs and your funders provide the capital. We make sure the plan is one every partner can sign.
