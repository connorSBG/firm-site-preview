Core module 07

Venture Capital Strategy

A clear answer on whether a fund is right for you and, if it is, the structure, terms, governance, and policies to do it well.

6 to 9 weeks for the decision memo and options analysis; 10 to 14 weeks for a full fund design.Stand It UpBuildBlueprint + Coach
What this solves

The first question is rarely fund or no fund. It is what to build first: a microgrant pool, a student-managed fund, a sidecar, or the fund itself. When a fund is right, the hard part is the partnership terms with the manager: economics back to your institution, investment committee seats, conflicts, donor access, and what students get.

When this fits
  • A trustee or donor has asked about starting a fund.
  • Your founders raise their first money elsewhere and you want some of that capital to be local.
  • An outside manager has proposed a partnership and you want to know what terms are normal.
  • You are considering an LP commitment and want a policy in place before you sign.
What we build with you
  1. "Do You Need a Fund?" Decision Memo
  2. Options Analysis (no fund, microgrants, student-managed fund, sidecar, fund-of-one, independent affiliated fund, LP commitments)
  3. Fund Thesis
  4. University or Institution Partnership Term Framework (economics to the institution, investment committee seats, exclusivity, donor access, student sub-fund)
  5. Investment Policy and Conflicts Policy
  6. Manager Selection Criteria
  7. LP Readiness Checklist

Legal formation and securities counsel are separate. We design the structure and coordinate with your counsel so the documents match.

Ask about this module

Our partners have designed and co-founded a university-affiliated venture fund, from fund structure and partnership agreement through fundraising and capital deployment. That structure returns a share of carried interest to the university, seats a joint university and manager investment committee, and includes a student sub-fund.

That experience is the basis for the partnership term framework. We do not manage your money. The decision memo is written for your institution. If the answer is not yet, the memo says so and lays out what comes before a fund.
