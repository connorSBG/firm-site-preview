Core module 02

Strategy and Center Design

The charter, structure, governance, plan, and budget for a center or office that will still make sense in three years.

6 to 10 weeks.Stand It UpBuildBlueprint + Coach
What this solves

A center that lasts needs more than a name and a director. It needs a reporting line everyone recognizes, a budget line of its own, and an agreed definition of success. A charter is what makes a center an institution rather than a project.

When this fits
  • Your center reports to a department and you want it reporting to a VP or provost.
  • You want a recurring budget line instead of an annual renegotiation from zero.
  • You want faculty in every college to see the center as theirs, not the business school's.
  • A new leader is about to be hired and you want the job description to come with a plan.
What we build with you
  1. Center or Office Charter
  2. Mission, Vision, and Theory of Change
  3. Organizational Model and Reporting Lines
  4. Governance and Advisory Board Design
  5. Three-Year Strategic Plan
  6. Annual Operating Budget Model
Ask about this module

A center launched inside one college and a center with a university-wide mandate differ in governance: where it reports, who sits on the advisory board, which units have decision rights, and how the budget is built. Our partners have led a center through exactly that elevation. This module writes all of it down before the first program launches, so the center is designed for where it is going, not only where it starts.
