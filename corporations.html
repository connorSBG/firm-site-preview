Who we serve

Corporations

Startup partnerships, an internal venture program, and a clear answer on corporate venture capital.

Where you might be

Your leadership wants exposure to startups, new products, or a corporate venture arm, and wants it without a large consultancy or an outsourced fund. Business units may already be trialing startup products. An innovation thesis may be drafted and waiting for a structure behind it. You want a front door, a process, and a decision about capital that fits the company you are.

Questions worth asking
  • Should we start a corporate venture arm, or something lighter first?
  • How do we work with startups as a customer and partner?
  • How do we run an internal innovation challenge or venture program that produces funded projects?
  • Should we partner with a university or research institution, and how?

When this fits

  • 01The board or CEO has asked for a corporate venture proposal.
  • 02A competitor announced a startup partnership and your board wants a point of view.
  • 03Your company has made an investment in a vendor or partner and wants a policy for the next one.
  • 04You have run an idea contest and want the next one to end in funded projects with owners.

What we build with you

Each item maps to a module with named deliverables.

  • Innovation thesis
  • Startup partnership and venture-client pilot program
  • Intrapreneurship challenge design
  • Corporate venture readiness assessment with thesis, governance, investment committee, and the balance sheet versus fund versus LP decision
  • University and research partnership strategy
Sample engagement

Example: a manufacturer exploring startup partnerships

Stand It Up6 to 9 weeks

A startup pilot program with sourcing, screening, a pilot agreement, and a scale decision, plus a corporate venture readiness memo that lays out what a venture arm would require and recommends the sequence.

The venture-client model, buying from startups as a customer with a light process, delivers most of the benefit of a corporate venture arm at a fraction of the cost and risk, and it tells you what you want from the market before you invest in it. When a venture arm is the right move, our partners have built and governed venture vehicles and can design the thesis, the committee, and the structure. Either way, you own the program and someone inside the company runs it.
