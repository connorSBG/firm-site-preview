Who we serve

Health Systems

An innovation office, a clinician invention pathway, a pilot program, and a venture decision, designed for your system.

Where you might be

Your clinicians have ideas, startups want to pilot with you, and your board has heard about health system venture arms. You want a front door for all of it, an invention policy written for clinicians, a pilot process with a path to a contract, and a deliberate position on capital, at a size and budget that fits your system.

Questions worth asking
  • Should we launch a venture fund, join one as an LP, or wait?
  • How should we handle clinician inventions?
  • How do we run startup pilots under a standard agreement with a path to a contract?
  • What does an innovation office look like at our size?
  • Can philanthropy or our foundation fund this?

When this fits

  • 01A clinician has commercialized something and leadership wants a pathway so the next one goes through the system.
  • 02Several startup pilots are running across departments and you want one list, one agreement, and one set of metrics.
  • 03An innovation alliance has invited your system to commit capital and you want an independent view.
  • 04Your foundation has a gift earmarked for innovation and you want a plan for it.
  • 05A board member has asked about a venture arm.

What we build with you

Each item maps to a module with named deliverables.

  • Innovation office charter and operating model
  • Clinician invention intake and triage
  • IP and revenue-sharing policy written for clinicians
  • Licensing versus spinout decision framework
  • Partner selection for outside tech transfer support
  • Startup pilot and sandbox program with a pilot agreement template and a pilot-to-contract gate
  • Venture strategy: direct, co-invest, fund-of-one, LP, or not yet
  • Philanthropy and foundation co-funding plan
Sample engagement

Example: a multi-hospital system building its first innovation office

Build4 to 6 months

An innovation office charter and staffing plan, a clinician invention policy and intake process, a startup pilot program with a standard agreement, and a venture decision memo for the board.

The forty largest US health systems report a median innovation budget around $3.5 million and about 9.5 staff. We design those as one system, sized to your budget. We do not manage capital. The venture memo recommends what is right for your system.
