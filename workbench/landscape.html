The landscape, with names

Named firms stay here. The public site describes categories only. Benchmark figures are from the build brief and should be re-sourced before any of them is quoted externally.

Public label: Hire a leader

Hire a leader

About $200K salary, $250K to $300K all in with benefits and space

Named firms
  • Executive search firms
  • Direct hires from peer institutions
What the buyer gets

A person

The catch

Hiring before you know what to build. One person rarely covers both programs and capital.

Benchmarks
  • Brief figure: roughly $200K salary, $250K to $300K all in.
Our note

Not a competitor so much as the thing we make work. We write the plan and the job description; the client hires into it.

Public label: Study or assessment firm

Study and assessment firms

About $25K to $120K per study

Named firms
  • TEConomy Partners
  • Tripp Umbach
  • Camoin Associates
  • University economic impact study vendors
  • Tech transfer specialists and interim TTO providers
What the buyer gets

A report and recommendations

The catch

Stops at recommendations. No operating documents, no playbooks, no policies.

Benchmarks
  • University economic impact studies have been bought for $25K to $50K.
  • A UK university's 4-month strategy project was about £88K.
  • A state innovation economy plan was budgeted $80K to $120K for about 4 months.
Our note

Closest category on price. Our difference is that the deliverables are operating documents, not findings. We can also follow a study firm's report with the build.

Public label: Operator or accelerator-as-a-service

Operators and accelerators-as-a-service

About $400K to $1.5M+ per year, or equity

Named firms
  • gener8tor
  • Plug and Play
  • Techstars (university and corporate programs)
  • Venture studios
  • Outsourced university fund managers
What the buyer gets

A program run for you

The catch

You rent it. Costly. Results questioned in some markets. The institution does not own the capability.

Benchmarks
  • A regional Plug and Play style innovation program has run about $1.5M per year.
  • An accelerator operator contract ran about $1.2M over 3 years.
Our note

Sometimes the right answer, and we say so. Our pre-accelerator at TU was run with a national partner. We help clients decide what to own and what to rent, and negotiate the partner terms.

Public label: Membership or network

Memberships, networks, and alliances

Annual dues, or $10M to $50M fund commitments for capital-gated alliances

Named firms
  • AVIA (health systems)
  • Advisory Board (health systems)
  • NACCE (community colleges)
  • Babson programs and faculty development
  • GCEC and USASBE (peer associations)
  • Capital-gated health innovation alliances
What the buyer gets

Content, peers, dealflow

The catch

Generic. Not built around your institution. Capital-gated alliances are out of reach for small and mid systems.

Benchmarks
  • Capital-gated health innovation alliances require large fund commitments.
  • The largest 40 US health systems report a median innovation budget around $3.5M with about 9.5 FTE.
Our note

Complementary. We recommend memberships where they fit. GCEC and USASBE are also where our proof points come from.

Public label: Fractional executive

Fractional innovation executives

Roughly $7.5K to $18K per month

Named firms
  • Independent fractional chief innovation officers
  • Fractional TTO directors
What the buyer gets

Part of a person, ongoing

The catch

Ongoing cost with no end state. Quality varies. Usually one half (programs or capital), not both.

Benchmarks
  • Fractional innovation executives run roughly $7.5K to $18K per month.
Our note

Reference point for our retainer pricing. Our retainer should sit below this range because it follows a fixed-fee design engagement rather than replacing a hire.

Public label: Boutique consultancy

Productized boutique consultancies

Paid diagnostic credited toward a larger engagement; fixed-fee sprints; monthly retainers with minimum terms

Named firms
  • Published-price strategy boutiques in adjacent fields (higher education strategy, innovation consulting, fund formation advisory)
What the buyer gets

Fixed-scope deliverables

The catch

Rarely combine programs and capital. Rarely built by people who ran the programs.

Benchmarks
  • Published-price boutiques typically offer a paid diagnostic credited toward a larger engagement, fixed-fee sprints, and monthly retainers with minimum terms.
Our note

This is the pricing model we are copying: Diagnostic with credit, fixed-fee tiers, retainer add-on. The difference is the subject matter and the practitioner history.
