Positioning canvas

One page we can argue with. Edit this file directly; it is deliberately not in a data collection so it reads as a document.

Target customer
  • Small and mid-sized universities and colleges (primary). The buyer is a provost, a dean, a VP for research or advancement, or the one faculty member carrying the effort.
  • Regional health systems with several hospitals and no innovation staff. Buyer: CMO, chief strategy officer, foundation director.
  • Mid-sized companies with a CEO or board asking about startups or CVC. Buyer: CEO, CFO, head of strategy.
Problem
  • They ask for the wrong thing first (a fund, an accelerator) because nobody has shown them the menu.
  • The big options are too big: operators, national partners, large consultancies.
  • Hiring is premature: $250K to $300K a year before the job is defined.
  • They lack a starting playbook and reinvent the same documents.
Alternatives
  • Hire a leader. Study or assessment firm. Operator or accelerator-as-a-service. Membership or network. Fractional executive. Do nothing.
Our difference
  • Designed by practitioners who did it: one built the programs, center, rankings, and donors; the other built the affiliated fund and the capital structure.
  • Programs and capital designed as one system.
  • Capital-neutral: we never manage the money, so the fund advice is honest.
  • Template-accelerated: documents already used in real programs.
  • Right-sized: weeks, fixed fees, two senior people.
  • Owned by you: every deliverable is the client's.
Proof
  • The University of Tulsa: center launched 2023, GCEC award 2024, Princeton Review Top 50 in 2025, $1.5M naming gift, university-wide in 2026, affiliated fund with carried interest to the university, joint investment committee, and student sub-fund.
  • Program library built from JOLT, Hurricane Pitch Competition, Cherokee AcceleratHER, high school Innovation Challenge, summer camp, microgrants, pre-accelerator.
  • AURP origin story: a dozen institutions asked for exactly this.
Objections and answers
  • Our board has already decided we are starting a fund. Then you need the partnership terms, the governance, the investment policy, and the pipeline to make it work, and you need them before counsel starts drafting.
  • We already have a consultant. Good.
  • Can't we just copy what TU did? You can start from it, which is what our library is.
  • We can figure this out ourselves. Many institutions do, over three to five years, by reinventing the same documents and making the same mistakes.
  • We could hire someone for that. You could, and you probably will.
One-line position
  • 6611 Partners: Designed by practitioners. Owned by you. We take you to the 5-yard line; your team scores.
