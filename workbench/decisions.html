DECISIONS.md

Running log of open decisions, judgment calls, and placeholders for the 6611 Partners site. Edit freely. The Workbench renders this file at /workbench/decisions. Section 11 is regenerated by npm run verify:report; edit everything else by hand.

Legend: Open needs a founder decision. Made is a judgment call Claude made during the build, which can be reversed. Locked is final.

1. Firm name and domain

Status: Name locked (Connor, 2026-10-07): 6611 Partners, set in site.config.ts. Domain still open; url is a placeholder. The twenty earlier candidates remain in src/content/names.json and at /workbench/brand for the record. No domain availability has been checked.

2. Primary audience to lead with on Home

Status: Made (reversible). Home leads with universities. Health systems and companies are the second and third audience cards. Rationale: the origin story, the proof, and the founders’ operating track record are strongest in universities, and the brief marks universities as primary.

3. Tier names

Status: Open. Current names follow the brief: Diagnostic, Stand It Up, Build, Blueprint + Coach.

Alternatives to consider:

  • Diagnostic / Launch / Build / Blueprint
  • Assess / Design / Build / Partner
  • Starting Point / One Thing Done / The Center / The Ecosystem
  • Diagnostic / Sprint / Build / Blueprint + Coach (keeps three, renames the second)

The weakest name is “Stand It Up.” It is plain and accurate but reads casual next to the others. “Sprint” is the most common replacement in the market but is overused.

5. Retainer and add-on pricing

Status: Open. All add-on prices are DECISION placeholders in src/content/addons.json.

  • Advisory retainer after any engagement: suggested range $3K to $8K per month. Compare: fractional innovation executives run roughly $7.5K to $18K per month.
  • Board or leadership workshop: half day or full day.
  • Rankings submission sprint (annual).
  • Donor or funder presentation support.

6. Hero headline choice

Status: Open. Showing option 1 (restored at Connor’s request after the copy pass changed it). All three are in src/data/hero.json.

  1. “Build the innovation program your institution actually needs.” (shown)
  2. “We get you to the 5-yard line. You score.”

Note: option 2 is the most memorable and the most informal. It may work better as a section heading on How We Work than as the first line a provost reads.

8. Venture fund metrics to publish

Status: Open. The public site describes the Hurricane Ventures structure only (carried interest share to the university, joint investment committee, student sub-fund). Capital raised, number of investments, and portfolio follow-on funding are tagged for verification and not shown as numbers. Decide whether to publish any of them once verified, and whether the fund’s own LPs would object.

9. Audience categories, and which to launch with

Status: Open. Connor’s note on 2026-10-07: the brief’s three audiences were examples, not the taxonomy. The build now uses four broad categories with no size qualifiers: Universities and Colleges (/universities), Health Systems (/health-systems), Corporations (/corporations), and Economic Development Organizations, Foundations, and Research Parks (/economic-development). Home adds “and organizations like them.” Questions to settle: are these the right four; should any be merged or renamed (for example “Research Institutions” alongside universities, or “Hospitals and Health Systems”); and which appear in the nav at launch.

10. Copy about the scope boundary

Status: Made (reversible). The brief’s “5-yard line / you score” framing and the “we do / we do not” manifesto are internal shorthand and no longer appear on public pages (Connor, 2026-10-07: some engagements will not go that far and some will go further). Public copy now says: we design it, your team runs it, engagements are scoped to what you need, some end at the design and some keep us close through the first year. The hard limits stay implicit in the offers and the FAQ (we do not act as program operator, fund manager, or interim director). Items reviewed during the build:

  • How We Work: “What we do / what we do not do” became “What we design / what your team owns” with a scope note.
  • FAQ “Will you run our program?” rewritten to describe the design scope and coaching rather than a flat refusal.
  • Home process section no longer promises “you own everything” as a slogan in every section; it appears once.

12. Other judgment calls made during the build

  • Stack: Astro 7, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, static output. Vanilla TypeScript for interactive islands; the only bundled library is three.js for the Home background.
  • Audience pages at top level URLs: /universities, /health-systems, /companies.
  • Proof page at /proof, one case for now.
  • Scoping tool at /start. Scoring logic shown only at /workbench/scoping.
  • Contact form is a mailto link with a prefilled subject. No form backend, no third-party form service.
  • Maturity model stages: Ad hoc, Emerging, Organized, Integrated, Nationally recognized. Dimensions: strategy and governance, curriculum, programs, commercialization, capital, funding and advancement, measurement.
  • Secondary audiences (economic development organizations, community foundations, research parks) get a paragraph on Home and a FAQ entry, not their own page.
  • Design language: translated from the reference template Connor supplied on 2026-10-07 (floating glass nav, Archivo headings with italic Playfair accent words, glass cards, staggered scroll reveals, typewriter hero, full-height section rhythm, animated FAQ, pill tags and ask-band footer, 3D helix background). Content, palette, and copy are ours.
  • Fonts: Archivo Variable (width axis) and Playfair Display Variable italic, self-hosted via fontsource rather than Google Fonts, so the site makes no external requests.
  • 3D background: three.js is bundled from npm (about 570 KB before compression, Home page only) rather than loaded from a CDN, to honor the no-third-party-scripts rule. It is skipped for visitors who prefer reduced motion and dimmed on narrow screens. Decision to keep it or drop it for weight is open.
  • Default palette changed to slate (the violet accent closest to the reference template). ink (green) and harbor (brass) remain one config value away and can be previewed at /workbench/brand.
  • Offers page has a toggle between list price and price after the Diagnostic credit, in place of the template’s monthly/yearly toggle.
  • The template’s testimonials section is omitted: we have no client quotes and will not fabricate any. The AURP origin story appears on the Team page instead.
  • VOICE.md (2026-10-07) is the public copy standard. Rules 11 to 16 record Connor’s second round of feedback: no internal shorthand on public pages, our methods are ours (never “already used at” an employer), do not sell the firm’s size, audiences are categories not examples, results are general, insights carry ideas not verdicts.
  • Results page (/results, formerly /proof) is anonymized: “a private university,” no program brand names, no gift or prize amounts, outcomes stated in magnitude words (seven-figure naming gift, bank-sponsored prize pool, national Top 50). The underlying data file is src/content/proof/flagship-center.json.
  • Partner bios keep employer names, titles, degrees, board seats, and the partners’ own companies as biographical facts, including The University of Tulsa (Connor, 2026-10-07: keep it). They no longer name programs, the fund, gift amounts, prize amounts, or the starting capital of Chris’s first company (removed at Connor’s request).
  • The handoff article carries no dollar figures (Connor, 2026-10-07); our prices and the market benchmarks live on Offers and the comparison table. Open: the health-system article still cites the forty-system median budget (tagged VERIFY) and the gift-menu article uses illustrative arithmetic (a $2,500 microgrant, a $30,000 program, an endowment sized to it).
  • Insights re-titled around ideas: Pipeline before capital; Design, then handoff; What it takes to reach a national ranking in three years; Founder-friendly IP policy, in plain English; A front door for startups and clinicians; Sizing the ask.
  • Playbook Library is framed as proprietary playbooks developed by the firm and refined with every engagement, never as documents used at a prior institution.
  • The FAQ “You are two people. Is that enough?” was removed under rule 13.
  • The Team page “Why” block no longer retells the conference anecdote (Connor, 2026-10-07: still too literal). It states the founding belief instead. VOICE.md rule 17 records the principle.
  • Preview published 2026-10-07 at https://connorsbg.github.io/firm-site-preview/ (public by link, noindex, robots disallow all). Static files only live in the preview repo; the source stays local. Redeploy with npm run deploy:preview. Open: which host and domain for launch.
