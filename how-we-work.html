How we work

We design it.
Your team runs it.

Strategy, structure, governance, policies, pathways, playbooks, budgets, KPIs, and staffing plans, designed with you and written for the people who will run them. Engagements are scoped to what you need: some end at the design, some keep us close through the first year.

What we design, and what
your team owns

The split below is the usual shape. Where the line falls is set in your scope document, and coaching can keep us close after launch.

We design
  • Strategy, structure, and governance
  • Policies: IP, licensing, conflicts, investment
  • Program playbooks and templates
  • Budgets, staffing plans, and job descriptions
  • KPI scorecards and rankings readiness
  • Fundraising strategy, case for support, and gift menus
  • Venture fund design and a clear answer on whether you need one
  • Working sessions with your team and optional coaching
Your team owns
  • Running the programs, competitions, and events day to day
  • Making the asks to donors and sponsors, with our case and gift menu in hand
  • Managing any capital, within the structure and governance we design
  • Submitting grants and rankings surveys, with our readiness kits
  • Hiring and leading the team, into the roles we define
  • Legal formation and securities work, with your counsel

Discover to Coach

Five steps. The first four happen in every engagement larger than a Diagnostic. The fifth is yours to add.

01

Discover

Interviews with leadership, faculty or clinicians, students or staff, advancement, and partners. Review of programs, policies, budgets, and data.

Interview synthesis and asset map

02

Diagnose

Score your institution across the seven dimensions of our maturity model. Benchmark against peers. Find the two or three moves that matter most.

Maturity gap map and roadmap

03

Design

Build the modules you chose: charters, playbooks, policies, budgets, staffing plans, scorecards, donor case, venture decision. Adapted from our proprietary frameworks to your institution.

Named, finished documents

04

Hand off

Working sessions with the team that will run it. Launch checklist. 12-month calendar. Leadership and board presentation.

A team ready to launch

05

Coach

Optional monthly coaching and quarterly reviews while your team executes. We adjust the plan as the first year unfolds.

First-year scorecard

Programs create the pipeline.
Capital follows.

The order matters. A fund needs ventures to invest in, and programs are where ventures come from.

Five principles

Sized to you

Built for institutions that want a serious program without a national partner or a full-time hire before the program exists. Engagements run in weeks, not years.

Proprietary playbooks

We start from frameworks, playbooks, and templates we developed and refine through practice. Your budget goes to judgment and fit, not to a blank page.

Capital-neutral

We do not manage your money. The fund recommendation is written for your institution and nothing else.

Built for handoff

Every deliverable is written for the person who will run it, with the detail that person needs on day one.

Clear about sequence

A Diagnostic often settles what comes first and what can wait. Order matters more than ambition.
