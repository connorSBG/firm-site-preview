Offers

Four tiers.
Fixed fees. You own it.

Start with a Diagnostic and know where to begin. Or go straight to the module you need. Every tier ends with documents your team can run. Coaching is available if you want us close through the first year.

The Diagnostic fee is credited in full toward a larger engagement signed within 60 days.

Start here

Diagnostic

Starting at
$10,000/ 2 to 3 weeks

Where you are today and what to build first.

  • Kickoff workshop with leadership and the people closest to the work
  • 6 to 12 stakeholder interviews
  • Review of existing programs, policies, budgets, and data
  • Peer benchmarking against 3 to 5 comparable institutions
  • Current-state assessment and gap map against our maturity model
  • Prioritized 12 to 24 month roadmap
  • Readout session with leadership

Best for: Institutions that want to invest in entrepreneurship and innovation and have not yet decided what to build first. Also the fastest way to decide whether a fund, a center, or a competition comes first.

100 percent of the Diagnostic fee is credited toward a Stand It Up, Build, or Blueprint + Coach engagement signed within 60 days.

Tier 2

Stand It Up

Starting at
$35,000/ 6 to 9 weeks

One program, policy, or decision, designed end to end.

  • One focus area (one module) designed end to end
  • Proprietary playbooks, templates, and policies, tailored to you
  • Budget and staffing plan for that area
  • Launch checklist and a 12-month calendar
  • Two working sessions with the people on your team who will run it
  • 30 days of email support after handoff

Best for: A specific near-term need: launching a pitch competition, a microgrant program, a student fellowship, a startup pilot program, or deciding whether to start a venture fund.

Ask about this tier
Tier 3

Build

Starting at
$90,000/ 4 to 6 months

A center, office, or program portfolio, designed as one system.

  • Everything in the Diagnostic
  • Integrated strategy across programs, policy, and capital
  • 3 to 4 modules designed end to end
  • Governance model and decision rights
  • Policy package
  • KPI scorecard
  • Staffing plan and job descriptions
  • Fundraising case and gift menu
  • Implementation roadmap
  • Leadership and board presentation

Best for: Institutions ready to stand up a center, an innovation office, or a portfolio of programs and that want them to work together from day one.

Tier 4

Blueprint + Coach

Starting at
$150,000/ 12 months

Or a Build fee plus a monthly coaching retainer

The full design, plus a year of coaching while your team executes.

  • Everything in Build, across the full set of modules relevant to you
  • Original research, such as an alumni founder survey and a venture potential model
  • Monthly coaching for your team as it executes the plan
  • Quarterly leadership reviews
  • First-year scorecard and plan adjustment

Best for: Institutions building a complete innovation and entrepreneurship ecosystem that want a partner close by through the first year.

Terms, in brief

Fixed fees
Not hourly. Each tier has a starting price and a defined scope. If scope grows, we agree on the change before the work.
Clear scope
We design. Your team runs it. The scope document says exactly which documents you will receive.
You own everything
Every deliverable is yours to use, adapt, and share inside your institution.
Third-party costs
Data subscriptions, survey incentives, travel, and legal fund formation are separate from our fee and billed at cost or paid directly by you.
