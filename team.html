Team

The partners.
Both sides of the work.

Our partners have built the institutional side (programs, curriculum, centers, rankings, donors) and the capital side (fund structure, investment governance, commercialization finance, institutional capital). Most advisors cover one.

Why 6611 Partners

Most institutions that decide to take innovation and entrepreneurship seriously are offered the same three paths: hire a leader before the role is defined, commission a study that ends at recommendations, or rent a program from an operator. Each one costs more than it should and leaves the institution without the thing it needs most, which is a plan it can run itself.

6611 Partners exists for the fourth path. We design the strategy, the programs, the policies, and the capital structure, drawing on the experience of partners who have built each of them, and we hand the finished work to the people who will run it.

We design it with you. Your team runs it. You own everything.
